# TPJS: what the public release may bundle, fetch, link, or index

The page text of *Teachings of the Prophet Joseph Smith* (TPJS) is public domain in the United States: the 1938 compilation was registered and never renewed. The citations are the obstacle. Every one of the 371 `T` sources is a TPJS page, and its cites are Richard C. Galbraith's scripture annotations: copyrighted new matter in *Scriptural Teachings of the Prophet Joseph Smith* (STPJS, Deseret Book, 1993). So **TPJS reaches the public release only with a cleared cite set.** A cite set clears in one of two ways: written permission to reuse Galbraith's annotations, or an index built independently from the public-domain text. Once a cite set clears, bundle the annotation-free 1938 text and keep the inline reader. Link out as the secondary reading destination. Until then, TPJS stays in the personal build.

Checked **October 5, 2026**, for [issue 53](https://github.com/danielbaldwin47/Translations-and-Citations/issues/53) (part of the [map, issue 52](https://github.com/danielbaldwin47/Translations-and-Citations/issues/52)), against repository commit `9f10ef0`. Labels, vocabulary, and method follow [issue-50-public-release.md](issue-50-public-release.md) and the `GLOSSARY.md` "Release" section. **Verified** means a primary record was read or a measurement was run on October 5. **Inferred** means a reading or consequence drawn from verified facts. **Unknown** means the evidence was not found. Every request was an unauthenticated public GET (or an OPTIONS preflight probe). This is a research record, not a legal opinion.

## Recommendation per option

The bar is the map's: a reasonable-basis reading suffices, and written permission is sought only where no such reading exists.

| Option | Recommendation | Rights evidence it rests on |
|---|---|---|
| **Index only** (ship BYU's `T` cites as a references-only index) | **Needs permission.** No reasonable-basis reading exists. The cite set is Galbraith's annotation apparatus, copied almost one-for-one (11,348 cites against 11,354 annotation links; see finding 3). He calls it judgment, "both direct and contextual", which is the allusion judgment the map's standing reading keeps out of the public release. Two routes clear it. (a) Written permission, from Galbraith and from Deseret Book Company, the 1993 claimant. (b) An independent index derived from the public-domain text: explicit references give about 150 cites (~1% of today's coverage), and verbatim-quotation matching against public-domain scripture texts would add an unmeasured number more. | Route (a): the permission letter. Route (b): findings 1 and 2 (the text is public domain; facts found in it are ours). |
| **Bundle the page text** | **Yes, once a cite set clears.** Ship the 1938 text with every Galbraith element removed: pages 1-3, the `footRef` markers, and the footnote lists. It keeps the inline reader and highlights. Without a cleared cite set, nothing opens it. | Finding 1: registration A 115513 was not renewed by its March 12, 1966 deadline, so the compilation (Joseph Fielding Smith's selection, arrangement, headings, notes) is public domain in the US. Finding 2: the underlying Joseph Smith texts are public domain on their own. Inferred: a faithful transcription of public-domain text adds no copyrightable expression, so BYU's HTML stripped of annotations is a usable source; the archive.org scan is the fallback source. |
| **Fetch live from BYU** | **Not recommended.** It works technically: `talks_ajax` sends `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`. But it returns the annotated page, adds a BYU dependency, and gives nothing the public-domain bundle lacks except the copyrighted footnotes. If the owner wants those footnotes shown, it is acceptable on the map's user-triggered-fetch reading, but it still needs a cleared cite set to drive it. | The user-triggered-fetch reading the map applies to modern conference talks. BYU's stance on `talks_ajax` use is the open BYU question (A1/A2 in the issue-50 register). |
| **Link out** | **Yes, as the secondary reading destination.** Prefer the public-domain scan page on archive.org; BYU's `stpjs.html#N` is the annotated alternative. A link alone still needs a cleared cite set to say which page to open. | A link is not a copy. The scan is public domain (findings 1 and 4). |

**The bottleneck is the cite set, not the text.** Every option above opens TPJS from a verse, and the only verse-to-page mapping we hold is Galbraith's. The owner decides: ask for annotation permission (one paragraph added to the pending BYU email, plus Deseret Book), fund an independent quotation-matching build, or keep TPJS in the personal build.

Inferred spec consequence: the reader's `bodyPassage` target ([ADR-0006](../adr/0006-stpjs-body-passage-stated-twice.md), `T` in `CORPUS_PLANS`) finds the cited passage through Galbraith's `footRef` markers. A stripped bundle needs passage anchors from whichever cite set ships.

## Finding 1: the 1938 compilation was registered and not renewed (Verified)

- **Registration.** Copyright Office card catalog record [A 115513](https://api.publicrecords.copyright.gov/search_service_external/simple_search_dsl?page_number=1&records_per_page=50&field_type=title&query=teachings%20of%20the%20prophet%20joseph%20smith&sort_order=asc&highlight=false&model=). Title *Teachings of the Prophet Joseph Smith*; claimant and author Joseph Fielding Smith; printed and published by The Deseret News Press; **first published March 12, 1938**; application and affidavit received March 18, deposit March 21. The application card ([front](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/copyright/hprcatcard/19/38/19/45/A_/11/52/00/-1/15/89/9/19381945A_115200-115899/CC19381945A_115200-115899.0313a.jpg), [back](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/copyright/hprcatcard/19/38/19/45/A_/11/52/00/-1/15/89/9/19381945A_115200-115899/CC19381945A_115200-115899.0313b.jpg)) was read and matches the record. The same entry appears in the *Catalog of Copyright Entries* 1938, Part 1, vol. 35 no. 4 ([NYPL transcription](https://github.com/NYPL/catalog_of_copyright_entries_project/blob/master/xml/1938/1938_v35_n4.xml)). A search of all 1938-1940 registrations for the title, "Fielding Smith", and "Deseret News Press" found only this entry, so the 1940 second edition was not separately registered.
- **Renewal deadline.** Under the 1909 Act the first term ran 28 years from publication, and renewal had to be filed in its final year. The deadline was therefore March 12, 1966. (Inferred legal reading on the verified date.)
- **No renewal.** NYPL's renewal transcriptions ([cce-renewals](https://github.com/NYPL/cce-renewals/tree/master/data), files `1963-1` through `1967-1`) hold no record for A 115513, the title, or a Joseph Fielding Smith TPJS claim. The filing window is covered: those files hold 397 (1965) and 156 (1966) renewals of other books first published in March 1938. Corroboration: OCR of the four *Catalog of Copyright Entries* renewal volumes for 1965 and 1966 ([Jan-Jun 1965](https://archive.org/details/catalogofcopy19653191lib), [Jul-Dec 1965](https://archive.org/details/catalogofcopy19653191libr), [Jan-Jun 1966](https://archive.org/details/catalogofco1966320112lib), [Jul-Dec 1966](https://archive.org/details/catalogofco1966320112libr)) has no title match. The OCR does find Joseph Fielding Smith's 1966 *new* registrations (*Answers to Gospel Questions* vol. 5, *Take Heed to Yourselves*). OCR recall measured on the known March 1938 renewal numbers was 78%, so the OCR result is corroboration, not proof.
- **Library determinations.** The HathiTrust Bibliographic API record for LCCN 38008207 ([JSON](https://catalog.hathitrust.org/api/volumes/full/lccn/38008207.json)) lists volume `mdp.39015020815562` with `rightsCode: "pd"`, Full view. archive.org item [`teachingsofproph00smit`](https://archive.org/details/teachingsofproph00smit) is the Second Edition, 1940, printed "Copyright 1938 by Joseph Fielding Smith". Its metadata reads "Copyright review: Public domain according to HathiTrust rights database".
- **Reading (Inferred).** The whole 1938 compilation has been public domain in the US since March 1966. That covers Joseph Fielding Smith's selection and arrangement, his section headings, and his editorial notes.
- **Contrast.** The heirs did renew some of his other books after 1978: [*Man, His Origin and Destiny*](https://api.publicrecords.copyright.gov/search_service_external/simple_search_dsl?page_number=1&records_per_page=100&field_type=name&query=Joseph%20Fielding%20Smith&sort_order=asc&highlight=false&model=) (RE0000144584), *Answers to Gospel Questions* vol. 5, and others. So the absence of a TPJS renewal is a gap in the record, not a family that never renewed anything.

## Finding 2: the underlying sermons and letters are public domain on their own (Verified dates, Inferred status)

- Joseph Smith died in 1844. TPJS draws on *History of the Church* vols. 1-6 (1902-1912), *Times and Seasons* (1839-1846), *Evening and Morning Star*, *Messenger and Advocate*, and manuscript sources (Manuscript History, Journal History, minutes), per its own abbreviation list. Works published before 1931 are public domain in the US. Passages first published in TPJS itself fall under finding 1.
- **Independent public-domain sources exist but are not needed.** archive.org holds *History of the Church* scans for every volume (for example [`historyofchurcho01robe`](https://archive.org/details/historyofchurcho01robe) through [`historyofchurcho06robe`](https://archive.org/details/historyofchurcho06robe)) and individual *Times and Seasons* issues (for example [`per_utah-and-the-mormons_times-and-seasons_1839-11_1_1`](https://archive.org/details/per_utah-and-the-mormons_times-and-seasons_1839-11_1_1)). English Wikisource has neither TPJS nor *History of the Church* beyond a single [A-1 page](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/History_of_the_Church/A-1) (API search, October 5). TPJS's own 1938 text is the simplest public-domain source for TPJS pages.
- **Joseph Smith Papers** publishes an [index from TPJS page to earliest source](https://www.josephsmithpapers.org/articles/sources-for-teachings-of-js-by-joseph-fielding-smith), organized by the six TPJS sections; row one is "Title Page of the Book of Mormon, p. 7, JS History, vol. A-1, 34". Its transcripts are "© 2026 by Intellectual Reserve, Inc." under the [Church terms of use](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/legal/terms-of-use?lang=eng&country=go) (JSP FAQ footer). So JSP is a reading destination for "original source", not a bundle source.

## Finding 3: BYU's STPJS is Galbraith's copyrighted edition, and the `T` cites are its annotations (Verified)

- **Registration.** Copyright Office [TX0003720598](https://api.publicrecords.copyright.gov/search_service_external/simple_search_dsl?page_number=1&records_per_page=20&field_type=title&query=scriptural%20teachings%20of%20the%20prophet%20joseph%20smith&sort_order=asc&highlight=false&model=). Title: "Scriptural teachings of the prophet Joseph Smith / selected and arranged by Joseph Fielding Smith; scriptural annotations and introd. by Richard C. Galbraith." Deseret Book Co., first published February 2, 1993, registered January 10, 1994. **Claimant: Deseret Book Company.** "New Matter: additions." 539 p.
- **BYU's copy.** [`stpjs.html`](https://scriptures.byu.edu/stpjs.html) (Last-Modified March 3, 2015; 395 `.page` divs, anchors `name="1"` to `"395"`) and [`STPJS.pdf`](https://scriptures.byu.edu/tpjs/STPJS.pdf) (395 pages, created 2008 by Liddle). Neither carries a copyright notice; the HTML says "First published in 1938." BYU's About page claims the site ("Copyright © 2010-2025, Richard C. Galbraith and Stephen W. Liddle"); see the issue-50 note.
- **What is Galbraith's** (measured by script over `stpjs.html`):
  - pages 1-3: title credit "With Scriptural Annotations By Richard C. Galbraith", dedication, "About the Author", "Author's Foreword";
  - 6,043 `<span class="footRef">` markers in the body;
  - 11,354 scripture links in the per-page `<div class="footnotes">` lists (about 189,000 characters, against about 812,000 characters of body text).

  Joseph Fielding Smith's own 1938 notes are the 28 lettered `textMark` / `textFootRef` notes, which are TPJS material.
- **The body is the 1938 text.** 141,608 of 145,555 five-word shingles (97.3%) of the STPJS body, with annotations removed, occur in the OCR of the 1940 scan. The 13 pages under 60% are Galbraith's pages 2-3 and the blank and section-divider pages. Pagination is TPJS's: STPJS p. 7 opens with "Title Page of The Book of Mormon", as JSP's index places it, and sampled passages on pp. 50, 163 and 250 sit beside matching running heads in the scan. The page match is Verified on the sample and Inferred for the rest.
- **The `T` cites are those annotations.** The repository's shards hold 11,348 cites to `T` talks (`src/citations/data/citations/*.json`, count by script), against 11,354 annotation links. `CLAUDE.md` ("STPJS markup differs") records that `T` citation spans sit in the footnote list. Galbraith's foreword: "the final count now stands at over 11,000 references, both direct and contextual", and "the line between scripture cited and scriptural concept noted became increasingly blurred". These are judgments, not facts read off the text.
- **What the public-domain text cites on its own** (Inferred, regex count over the annotation-free body): about 92 explicit chapter:verse references (for example "Romans 11:25", "Rev. 22:17", "3 Nephi 20:22") and about 50 chapter or section references. That is about 1% of the current `T` coverage. Verbatim quotations without a reference are more numerous but unmeasured.
